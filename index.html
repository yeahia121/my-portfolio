About

The person behind the projects

03+ Years building

I'm Yeahia Barbhuiya — founder of Gyan Abhyas, a creator building educational and motivational content across English, Bengali and Hindi. I'm also a second-year undergraduate student, which keeps me close to the exact audience I design and build for: learners figuring things out one topic at a time.

My work sits at the intersection of teaching, design and technology — scripting lessons, shaping how they look, and building the platforms that carry them. I care less about chasing trends and more about making things that are genuinely useful to the person on the other end.

What I do

Educational content, web design, digital products and community-driven learning experiences.

What drives me

Turning complex ideas into something a beginner can actually hold onto and use.

Where I'm headed

Growing Gyan Abhyas into a full digital learning platform while sharpening my craft as a builder.
